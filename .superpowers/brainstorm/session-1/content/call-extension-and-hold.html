2

Billed: 4 Credits • 1 Credit returned to wallet
3

Call Passes 5 Minutes

At 4:45, system checks if wallet has more credits:

✓ Has Credits: Auto-extends hold (+5 credits) seamlessly.
✗ Zero Balance: Agent gracefully wraps up and disconnects at 5:00.
